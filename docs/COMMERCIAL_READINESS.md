# DataLens commercial readiness

**Status:** controlled demonstration only; not approved for paid, public or organizational production use.

DataLens provides deterministic CSV profiling, exact-byte fingerprinting and portable signed workflow events. These controls support traceability inside a demonstration. They do not prove source truth, create an audit opinion, certify compliance or provide a durable organizational system of record.

## Release blockers

- Identify the contracting operator and publish counsel-approved privacy terms, product terms and organizational controller/processor terms.
- Define subprocessors, processing regions, transfer safeguards, retention/deletion, rights handling and incident cooperation.
- Add a repository licence/notice, establish code and asset chain of title, and publish a reviewed third-party dependency licence inventory and versioned SBOM.
- Staff support and security contacts, incident escalation, recovery testing and measurable service targets. No SLA or availability guarantee is currently approved.
- Implement approved durable audit storage, retention and independent verification before positioning approval events as an organization record.
- Validate packaging, willingness to pay and unit economics with real research. No price, revenue, adoption, retention or uptime claim is approved.

## Safe demonstration rules

Use only `samples/demo.csv` or another clearly labelled synthetic dataset. Describe the quality score as a deterministic profile of the submitted bytes. State that the uploader remains responsible for authority, legal basis, business definitions and material decisions. Refer to approval as “internal workflow approval” and retain the non-certification disclaimer in the UI and export.
