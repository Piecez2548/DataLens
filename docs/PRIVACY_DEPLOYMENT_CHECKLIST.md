# Privacy deployment checklist

This is an implementation checklist, not a privacy notice or legal opinion. Do not enable personal-data uploads until actual operator facts are supplied and approved.

- Controller legal name, address, contact, and DPO/contact where applicable
- Processing purposes and lawful basis for each intended dataset/use case
- Data categories, sources, recipients, subprocessors, countries, transfers, and safeguards
- Retention periods for CSV request handling, identity, platform, security, and audit records
- Rights request, correction, deletion, objection, complaint, identity verification, and escalation process
- Controller/processor agreements and written processing instructions
- Security controls, access review, incident owner, processor escalation, breach-risk assessment, 72-hour decision tracking, and notification templates
- ROPA/processing inventory and DPIA/sector assessment where required
- Thai counsel/DPO approval with date, scope, assumptions, and expiry/review date
